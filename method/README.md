# method/

Written method for building, keeping, and publicly releasing a small sovereign
multi-model AI fabric. These are the transferable lessons, de-identified and
told in plain vocabulary. Estate specifics, private logs, and raw evidence do
not appear here.

## The shelf

- `mining-a-private-estate.md`. How private work was swept for public release
  in waves, the gates it had to pass, and what the campaign taught. The fullest
  entry on this shelf; start here.
- `assumption-register.md`. How to write a plan a reviewer can attack: one
  objective, hard constraints, a numbered falsifiable assumptions register,
  named costs, excluded options, and attack prompts.
- `adversarial-review-reply.md`. The companion: how to review such a plan as a
  peer, the failure classes to hunt, and how to answer attack prompts without
  smoothing the disagreements away.
- `session-handoff-protocol.md`. Short and prescriptive. How to carry work
  across sessions on written state, and what every handoff must contain.
- `fail-visibly-postmortem.md`. The sunset of a silent background sweep, and
  the rules that follow from it: persist failures, count the repeats, give a
  job a progress signal that can stand still, and alert on cost not absence.
- `syslog-flood-postmortem.md`. A logging flood, sanitized. Rate-limit the
  loud path and watch the rate, not the total.
- `parallel-recon-report-method.md`. Producing an honest snapshot of a fast
  workspace: parallel read-only readers, live verification, disk over map,
  labeled inference.

## The through lines

Every claim carries a receipt. Authority lives in files. Rebuild contaminated
material rather than scrubbing it. Verify the scrub with a second stack, never
by self-attestation. Name what you held back, and why.

Floor: no em dashes, no ellipses.
